Enter all five values for an annual scenario estimate.
Formula: (input tonnes × disposal cost + recovered tonnes × substitute value − monthly processing/logistics) × 12. Gross scenario only. Confirm specifications, yield, permits, logistics and actual contracts before using it for decisions.

THE DISCOVERY PIPELINE

Not just a search bar.
A material-chain map.

When enabled, a server-side model extracts stated facts. Deterministic graph retrieval and matching find candidate routes, then qualification checks show what a person must verify.

01Extract

Optional AI structures only the facts stated in a listing and flags missing fields.

02Retrieve

Search the curated material graph for direct and process-dependent pathways.

03Rank

Compare specs, broad region, and direct volumes when the units are comparable.

04Qualify

Show route sources, uncertainty, safety and buyer checks for human review.
